Step 1

Download your connections from LinkedIn

Takes about 2 minutes, plus up to ~10 minutes for LinkedIn to prepare the file.

  1. 1

    Open LinkedIn's “Download your data” page

    Sign in to LinkedIn on a computer, then open the page directly:

    Open “Get a copy of your data” ↗

    Or go there by hand: click your photo Me ▾ (top right) → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy (left menu) → Get a copy of your data.

  2. 2

    Select only “Connections”

    Choose “Want something in particular? Select the data files you're most interested in.” and tick Connections. This is much faster than the full archive.

    Download larger data archive…
    Want something in particular? Select the data files…
    Articles✓ ConnectionsImported ContactsMessagesInvitationsProfile
    Request archive
  3. 3

    Click “Request archive” and confirm your password

    LinkedIn will email you when it's ready, usually within 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Download and unzip

    Use the link in the email, or go back to the same page and click Download archive. Unzip the file and find Connections.csv. That's the file you'll upload next.

    Don't edit the file. The few “Notes” lines at the top are expected.
